plotDigit = function(x) {
  cols = gray.colors(100, start = 0, end = 1, rev=TRUE)
  matObj = matrix(x,nrow=28)[,28:1]
  mode(matObj) = 'numeric'
  levelplot(matObj,col.regions=cols, xlab = '', ylab = '')
}

Answer 1.3

Describe this image by identifying pixels that seem to be positively associated with Y = ‘X5’ and positively associated with Y = ‘X8’.

The elastic net model identifies important pixels through the magnitude and sign of their estimated coefficients. Because digit 5 is treated as the positive class, positive coefficients increase the predicted log-odds of digit 5, while negative coefficients increase the relative evidence for digit 8. Coefficients close to zero contribute relatively little to the prediction, and coefficients equal to zero have been excluded by regularization. The important pixels represent locations where the shapes of digits 5 and 8 differ.

Answer 2.1

What values of degree and nprune are selected by maximizing CV accuracy?

The five-fold cross-validation selected a MARS/FDA model with degree = 3 and nprune = 50. The degree parameter allows interactions among up to three predictors, while nprune controls the maximum number of terms retained in the model.

The cross-validation plot shows that degree 3 consistently achieves higher accuracy than degrees 1 and 2. Accuracy improves as the number of terms increases from 10 to 50, but there is little additional improvement beyond 50 terms. The selected model achieves approximately 96.1% cross-validation accuracy, suggesting that nonlinear interactions among pixels are useful for distinguishing handwritten digits 5 and 8.
